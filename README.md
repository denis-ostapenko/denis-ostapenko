# Denis Ostapenko

[Website](https://denisostapenko.com/) · [Personal](https://denisostapenko.com/personal) · [Books](https://denisostapenko.com/books)

My experience is in cleaning services, industrial production and logistics. I look at how work is organized, where it gets stuck and what needs to change so people know who is responsible for what. At work, I value calm conversations and agreements that people follow through on. Now I apply AI and automation to these same practical tasks.

Outside work, I read, work on old motorcycles, enjoy fishing, train at the gym and follow American football.
